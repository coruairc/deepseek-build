<objective>
Stop the sandbox aborting when a runtime-socket deny candidate sits under a parent the sandbox process
cannot traverse, without widening the fail-closed predicate by one inch (SBOX-01), and prove it with a
fixture matrix plus an unmodified `deny_paths_e2e` (SBOX-02, test half).

Purpose: RESEARCH 1b proves the locked "skip when `canonicalize(parent)` is EACCES" predicate covers only
the ancestor-unsearchable shape. The `/run/containerd` shape — the parent IS the unsearchable directory —
has a *successful* `realpath` and the EACCES arrives from the endpoint `lstat`. Both shapes are "parent
not traversable" in the requirement's words, and only a second, narrow branch makes the fix real.

Output: a pure classifier, a `parent.join(".")` traversal probe, six-shape fixture matrix, and a devbox
scenario that has a real deny obligation on a host without `/data`.

mandated order; no artifact of 01-04 is used here. Do not commit.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| host filesystem -> deny policy | `/run`, `/var/run` and the user's home are attacker-influenceable paths; a resolution failure here decides whether a socket is masked |
| deny policy -> bwrap re-exec | the materialized set is the only thing bound over inside the namespace |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-01 | Tampering | `materialize_runtime_socket_deny_paths_from` | medium | mitigate | Skip only when the parent is provably not traversable (the `parent` plus `/.` lstat probe fails); the symlink, `InvalidInput` and dedupe arms stay byte-identical, so a maskable endpoint is still bound over. |
| T-01-02 | Elevation of privilege | skipping a maskable socket | medium | mitigate | A `PermissionDenied` from the endpoint under a *traversable* parent (an LSM denial) stays fatal, pinned by `endpoint_error_classification_table`; the predicate never treats EACCES as NotFound. |

| T-01-04 | Information disclosure | skip log content | low | accept | One `tracing::debug!` carrying the candidate path and nothing else; no env, no user data, no stderr noise. |
